Questions & Answers
How is your data sourced?
We use reputable publicly available sources, including utility connections, press releases, annual reports, bill processors, and more, to ensure our data’s reliability.
How do you keep your data accurate?
We maintain the accuracy of our data through regular updates and verification processes backed by a dedicated team of researchers.
How can I use Salesgenie data?
Salesgenie’s data offers versatile applications, including targeted marketing, lead generation, market research, and customer segmentation. It enables businesses to make informed decisions and drive growth.
What is data append or data enhancement?
Data append or enhancement is a process that involves adding missing or updating existing information in your customer database, enhancing its quality and completeness.
