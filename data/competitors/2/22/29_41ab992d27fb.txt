Frequently asked questions
What makes SalesIntel different from traditional intent providers?
Most intent providers capture late-stage signals when prospects are already evaluating competitors. SalesIntel tracks both predictive signals that reveal future intent, funding, leadership changes, hiring trends, and demand-capture signals showing active evaluation. Our proprietary AI suppression filters out 800M+ false positives, so you only see signals that are actionable. The result is earlier, more relevant outreach before competitors even know an account is in-market.
How does the AI-Native GTM Platform work end-to-end?
The platform follows a five-step workflow: Capture (detect buying signals via Signal360), Qualify (validate against your ICP with ICPIntel), Build (map the buying committee), Generate (create AI-personalized messaging for each stakeholder), and Engage (push activities into your CRM and trigger ProspectConnect outreach, automated or handed to reps for review). Each step feeds the next, so when a signal is detected, the right message is ready for the right person.
Do I need a data science team or technical resources to get started?
No. GTMCanvas uses a drag-and-drop interface that any GTM team member can use without writing code. ICPIntel requires only a list of your best customer accounts to build an initial model. Most teams are running their first signal-triggered workflow within days of setup.
How does SalesIntel handle data accuracy and signal quality?
SalesIntel uses a combination of AI and human verification to maintain 95% B2B contact data accuracy. On the signal side, our proprietary PredictiveIntent model applies a 60+ confidence threshold and suppresses 800M+ false positives, filtering out illogical matches based on technographic overlap, company size, and industry. Only high-confidence, actionable signals reach your team.
Which CRM and sales engagement platforms does SalesIntel integrate with?
SalesIntel integrates natively with Salesforce and HubSpot for CRM sync, Outreach and Salesloft for sales engagement, Marketo for marketing automation, and social media Ads and AdsIntel for account-based advertising. API and webhook access is available for custom integrations with your existing GTM stack.
How is SalesIntel Platform priced?
SalesIntel is available in tiers based on team size, signal volume, and feature needs. Core platform features including data, ICPIntel, and Signals are available across plans. GTMCanvas workflow automation, AI-Generated Content, and AdsIntel are available on higher tiers. Contact our team for a custom quote based on your GTM motion.
