Frequently Asked Questions
Pipeline generation, answered
What is a pipeline generation platform?
A pipeline generation platform combines the data, timing signals, and activation tools a go-to-market team needs to create qualified pipeline, in one system. Instead of buying a contact database, an intent provider, and an outreach tool separately, teams run ICP modeling, buying signal detection, and multi-channel activation off a single verified dataset.
What is the difference between predictive signals and intent data?
Intent data shows that an account is already evaluating a category, usually through topic surges or website behavior. Predictive signals show that an account is about to enter a buying cycle, through events like funding rounds, leadership changes, hiring surges, or new technology adoption. Intent data tells you to move now. Predictive signals give you weeks or months of lead time.
How accurate is SalesIntel's B2B contact data?
SalesIntel guarantees 95% accuracy on AI+Human-verified contacts and refreshes records on a 90-day cycle. Records are built by merging public, partner, and proprietary sources, then verified by a team of more than 2,000 researchers. Research On Demand covers any contact or account not already in the database.
What is an ICP fit score and how is it calculated?
An ICP fit score is a quantified measure of how closely an account resembles the customers a company already wins. SalesIntel’s ICPIntel builds the score by analyzing closed-won accounts across multiple dimensions including revenue, geography, headcount, departments, industry, technology stack, and keywords. It returns a dimension-by-dimension breakdown rather than a single opaque number.
What is a buying committee, and why does it matter for pipeline generation?
A buying committee is the group of people inside an account who must agree before a B2B purchase closes, typically an economic buyer, a champion, a technical evaluator, one or more influencers, and a blocker such as procurement. Buying signals fire at the account level, not the contact level, so pipeline generation only works when outreach reaches every seat rather than a single inbox.
How does SalesIntel compare to ZoomInfo, Apollo, and Cognism?
All four provide B2B contact and company data. SalesIntel differs in three ways: contacts are AI+Human-verified with a 95% accuracy guarantee rather than sourced through automation alone; buying signals include predictive events such as funding and leadership changes alongside standard intent topics; and implementation, support, and pipeline consulting are included in the contract. Teams evaluating alternatives typically test accuracy on a sample of their own target accounts before committing.
