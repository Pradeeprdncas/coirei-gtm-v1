Questions about the CRM.
Is the CRM really free?
Yes. It's included on every plan, Free too, and it never costs a credit. Credits pay for research, drafting, enrichment and calling.
Do I have to switch off HubSpot or Salesforce?
No. Connect it on any plan and your records sync in, with a backfill of what's already there. Switch on write-back and RegieGO's work flows back too. Each workspace connects one CRM.
Does it track deals and pipeline stages?
No. It's built for prospecting: people, accounts, tasks, lists and notes. Deals belong in the CRM your team runs them in, and RegieGO syncs with HubSpot and Salesforce for exactly that.
Can I bring in the contacts I already have?
Yes. Upload a CSV and map its columns, including to custom fields you create, or connect HubSpot or Salesforce.
Does my team see the same records?
Yes. People and accounts are shared across your workspace. Each person's tasks and chats stay their own.
