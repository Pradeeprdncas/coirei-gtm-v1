Questions about research and messaging.
Where do the signals come from?
RegieGO researches each account on the web and keeps what it can date and cite: funding, hiring, leadership moves and org changes. Each one links to its source.
Will it send anything without me?
Not unless you tell it to. You approve drafts, edit them, or ask for changes in chat, and an agent you set up can be told to wait for your approval on every send.
Will AI-written email hurt my domain?
Emails send from your own mailbox at a human pace: a few minutes apart, 20 a day by default, inside business hours, with do-not-contact and opt-out checks on every send.
Can it use our messaging and proof points?
Yes. Upload your own documents and save reusable writing instructions, and RegieGO draws on them every time it writes.
Does it automate LinkedIn?
No. RegieGO writes the message and opens the profile, and you send it from your own account.
