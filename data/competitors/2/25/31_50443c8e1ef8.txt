How does RegieGO know my ICP?
RegieGO reads your website and suggests the companies and roles to target. Refine your ICP in chat, and it finds matching accounts and the right buyers inside them.
Does RegieGO work with my existing tools?
Yes. You send from your own connected Gmail or Outlook. RegieGO comes with its own built-in CRM, so you can start with nothing, or connect HubSpot or Salesforce and your records sync in. Switch on write-back and RegieGO’s work flows back too.
What do I get in 10 minutes?
Give RegieGO your website and it suggests who to target, finds matching buyers, enriches their contact details, and prepares emails and call scripts in your pitch and voice, ready for you to review and use.
Can I work with accounts I already have?
Yes. Bring your target accounts or a prospect list into RegieGO, and it finds the right people at each account and enriches their contact details.
Does RegieGO find contact details?
Yes. RegieGO finds buyers’ emails and phone numbers through waterfall enrichment, checking up to seven data providers in turn for every contact. The email is verified before anything sends, you only pay when a provider finds something, and every record shows where the data came from.
Can I make calls from RegieGO?
Yes. Calling is built in, with single-line, power, and parallel dialing up to five lines at once, and RegieGO provisions local numbers for you. Research, contact details, call scripts, and previous activity stay in front of you as you dial. Calling is part of the Pro and Team plans.
Can I change the emails and call scripts?
Yes. Approve them as they are, edit them yourself, or tell RegieGO in chat what you’d like to change. Nothing sends or dials unless you tell it to.
Why use RegieGO instead of a database and a writing assistant?
A database gives you contacts and a writing assistant gives you drafts, but you’re the one stitching them together and keeping it running. RegieGO handles the whole motion in one place: it builds your list, enriches and verifies contacts, researches reasons to reach out, drafts in your voice, and dials.
Is RegieGO for individual sellers or teams?
Both. Pro is for one person. Team is priced per seat with a shared credit pool, and the workspace owner adds seats as you grow. Free holds up to 10 people.
