I can build this in Claude
You can. But you won't. Building it means prompt engineering, data connectors, CRM integration, context management, memory across sessions, workflow orchestration, and ongoing maintenance. That's a 3–6 month engineering project minimum. Salesworx is live in days. And once built, who maintains it when prompts drift, models update, or your sales process changes? We do that for you, continuously.Salesworx gives you production-ready AI sales infrastructure — not a prototype.
I have Salesforce and they are bringing agents inside — why do I need Salesworx?
Salesforce Agentforce is CRM-native, which means it optimises what's already in Salesforce. It doesn't do outbound prospecting, multi-channel sequencing, or account intelligence from signals outside the CRM. It also costs significantly more and requires Salesforce admin and AI expertise to configure. Salesworx plugs into Salesforce and fills the gap it leaves — the top-of-funnel, signal-driven, outreach layer.Agentforce manages your pipeline. Salesworx builds it.
I can personalise my emails and account research myself in Claude
Yes, for one account at a time. Salesworx does it for 50 accounts simultaneously, triggered automatically by signals, connected to your CRM, and logged without manual copy-paste. The ROI isn't in the quality of one email — it's in doing it at scale, consistently, without your reps spending 40 minutes per account on research they forget to save.One email in Claude is a task. Salesworx makes it a repeatable, scalable motion.
It will consume LLM tokens and gets expensive
We've designed Salesworx with token efficiency at the core — smart context windowing, cached account memory, and tiered model routing (lighter models for low-complexity tasks, stronger models only where needed). Our BYOLLM option lets you use your own API keys at cost. In practice, the token cost per account per month is a fraction of what one SDR hour costs. The math is straightforward.Token cost is a line item. SDR cost is a salary. We're replacing the second with the first.
I can build this in-house — better long-term TCO
Only if you have the AI engineers, sales domain expertise, and ongoing R&D budget to match a focused product team. In-house builds typically cost ₹50–80L in Year 1 (engineering + infra + ops), take 6–9 months to reach production quality, and then need continuous maintenance. Salesworx is subscription-based, always current, and built by people who only think about AI sales tooling. Most in-house builds get deprioritised when the team has other product roadmap pressure. We don't.Build vs buy is a resource question. Most teams don't have the resource — and find out late.
There are so many AI SDRs now — why Salesworx?
Most AI SDRs (AiSDR, Artisan, 11x) are autonomous outbound bots — spray-and-pray with AI copy. Salesworx is a sales orchestration platform, not just an SDR bot. It supports your human reps with intelligence, prioritisation, account context, and multi-channel coordination. It's built for complex B2B sales with longer cycles, multiple stakeholders, and high-value deals — not volume outreach to cold lists. Your reps stay in control; Salesworx makes them significantly faster and better informed.AI SDRs replace reps. Salesworx makes your reps irreplaceable.
I have HubSpot with Breeze AI
Breeze is a marketing automation layer — it optimises campaigns, nurtures inbound leads, and scores contacts already in your funnel. Salesworx operates upstream and alongside it. We handle signal-triggered outreach, account intelligence, multi-stakeholder mapping, and rep-assist workflows that Breeze doesn't touch. Breeze manages leads you already have. Salesworx helps you identify, prioritise, and engage accounts before they're in your funnel. They're complementary, not competitive.Breeze converts. Salesworx sources and prioritises — further up the funnel.
How will it communicate two-way with my CRM?
Native bi-directional sync via API connectors. Salesworx reads from your CRM — account data, contact history, deal stages, activity logs — and writes back to it — call notes, email interactions, engagement scores, next steps, and AI-generated summaries. No manual data entry, no parallel systems. Your CRM stays the system of record. Salesworx is the intelligence and action layer on top of it. Salesforce and HubSpot connectors are built-in. Others on request.Your CRM stays clean. Salesworx keeps it current automatically.
Will it generate leads for me or something else?
Salesworx doesn't sell you lead lists. What it does is help you convert your target accounts faster and with less effort. You bring your ICP and prospect list — Salesworx enriches those accounts with signals, scores them by readiness, personalises outreach, and helps your reps engage at the right time with the right message. If you need lead generation on top of that, Worxwide's ABM practice can layer that in as a managed service. But Salesworx itself is a sales execution and rep productivity platform, not a lead gen tool.We don't sell contacts. We help you close the ones you're already chasing.
AI agents are becoming commodity — new Llama models keep coming. What's Salesworx's moat?
The model is never the moat. The moat is three things. First, sales context and memory — Salesworx builds a persistent intelligence layer around your accounts, relationships, and deal history that gets smarter over time. A new Llama model doesn't have that context. We do. Second, workflow and integration depth — the value isn't AI generating text, it's AI embedded into the exact moment a rep needs it, connected to CRM data, triggered by signals, and acting within a defined sales process. Third, we ride model improvements, we don't compete with them — when Llama 4 or GPT-5 gets better, Salesworx gets better automatically. We're model-agnostic by design. Commoditisation of LLMs is good for us — better output at lower cost, passed directly to users.The moat is context, workflow, and integration depth. Models are just the engine.
