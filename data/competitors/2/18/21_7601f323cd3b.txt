The Problem with ABM today
ABM done poorly is just outbound with a nicer name on the deck.
Lists built on guesswork. Sales and marketing running disconnected programmes at the same accounts. No shared intelligence. No coordination. No measurable outcome tied to revenue.
SalesWorx.ai builds deep account profiles on every target — covering financials, growth signals, buying group composition, technology stack, competitive signals, and fit scores — before a single touchpoint is made. Coordinated, multi-channel campaigns are then orchestrated across every channel your target accounts use, adapting in real time to each account’s engagement signals.
Sales and marketing work from the same account data, the same scoring model, and the same playbooks. No misaligned handoffs. No duplicate outreach. No spray-and-pray disguised as ABM. And for organizations that need strategic depth alongside the platform, Worxwide operates as the human ABM extended team — senior practitioners embedded in your programmes, not an agency at arm’s length.
