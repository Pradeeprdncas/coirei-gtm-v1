The Problem with outbound today
Most outbound fails because it mistakes volume for strategy.
Generic sequences blast the same five templates to thousands of contacts and call it personalization. Buyers have learned to filter it.
SalesWorx.ai builds a live intelligence profile for every prospect before a single message is sent — pulling firmographic data, growth signals, leadership changes, technology stack, and real-time intent signals from across the web. The platform uses that context to generate outreach anchored to actual events in each prospect’s world, not variables dropped into a template.
The result: your reps execute 300+ personalized, signal-based touches per day without writing a single message from scratch — and without sending anything that reads like it came from a machine. Simultaneously, AI SDR agents handle replies, overcome objections, and book meetings 24/7, freeing your team entirely for conversations that require human judgment.
