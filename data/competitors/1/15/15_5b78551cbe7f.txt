How To Use
Wash hands before use. Insert applicator, push inner tube to release tampon. Change every 4-8 hours. Never leave in for more than 8 hours to reduce TSS risk.
Tampons are associated with Toxic Shock Syndrome (TSS), a rare but serious illness. Use the lowest absorbency for your flow and change every 4 to 8 hours. Never leave a tampon in for more than 8 hours. Remove the tampon and seek medical help right away if you develop a sudden high fever, vomiting, diarrhoea, a sunburn-like rash, dizziness or fainting.
