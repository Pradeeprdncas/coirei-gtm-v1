DEFYING NORMS IS IN OUR BLOOD
How we're reinventing menstruation for people everywhere.
OUR MISSION
To help people with periods thrive
Menstrual products are just the beginning. We believe in a world where vagina isn't a bad word, where all menstruators are part of the conversation, and where nobody is turned away from essential period care.
"DON'T BLAME YOUR BODY.
For my whole life, I accepted that my period meant discomfort. My body wasn’t to blame; my products were. I started Flex so anyone with a period could find comfort."
LAUREN SCHULTE WANG
Founder & CEO
OUR IMPACT
Better for you & the planet
MORE FLEX = LESS WASTE
Flex Reusable Disc and Flex Cup are zero-waste products, while Flex Disc and Flex Plant+ Disc reduce waste by
60-71%
compared to traditional period products. Period care that makes an impact.
MADE LOCALLY
Flex Discs and Flex Cup are made in the USA and Canada. Because our production facilities are so close to our warehouses, we reduce energy usage and emissions that come from transit.
SOURCED RESPONSIBLY
Our materials aren't just premium quality, they're also ethically sourced and repurposed when possible. Our factory partners at the heart of our business, where they are treated with care and respect.

Fighting stigmas, one period at a time.
