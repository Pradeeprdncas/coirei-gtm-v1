Flex Cup
Flex Cup is a reusable menstrual cup made from body-safe, medical-grade silicone, and the only cup with an adjustable, patented pull-tab that makes removal as easy as a tampon. That single feature removes the biggest barrier for beginners switching from tampons to a cup. Wear it for up to 12 hours of leak protection, day or night. One cup lasts up to 10 years, replacing years of disposable period products. Comfortable, low-maintenance, and kinder to your wallet and the planet.
Comes with a carrying pouch, plus 2 free single-use Flex Discs
; FSA & HSA eligible.
Size 1:
Capacity of 2 super tampons (22mL) and for first-time menstrual cup users and pros with light or heavy flows. 43mm x 46mm.
Size 2:
Capacity of 3 super tampons (30mL) and for experienced menstrual cup users who prefer a larger size or who have had 2+ vaginal births. 46mm x 53mm.
