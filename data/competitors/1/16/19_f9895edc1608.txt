A new direction
Focused. Forward. Together.
We’re working to protect nature’s diversity and the well-being of all life, now and for the future. With input from more than 2,000 supporters, including donors, staff and external experts, the David Suzuki Foundation board of directors developed a new strategic plan to guide our work. It’s based on three imperatives:
We must act with focus to address root causes of the environmental crisis.
We must act together to benefit from the multiplier effect of collective action.
We must act now before it’s too late.
Our vision is that we all act every day on the understanding that we are one with nature. We seek solutions that involve everyone, including you.
